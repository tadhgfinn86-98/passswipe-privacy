Talk it through

Questions.

What exactly do you need from me?
One recent waste invoice and a few photos of your current setup — the bins, the containers, where they live. That's enough for us to start.
Is the audit really free?
Yes. It costs you nothing and commits you to nothing. If we can't improve on what you have, we'll tell you that and you keep the report.
Are you a waste company?
No. Ripple is a broker. We source licensed waste operators on your behalf and work independently from them, so we're not pushing whatever service happens to be available.
Do I have to switch suppliers?
Only if you want to. The audit shows you what you're paying and what could be improved. What you do next is entirely your decision.
What about my existing contract?
We'll look at where you stand — notice periods, renewal dates, rollover terms — as part of the audit, so nothing gets triggered by accident.
Who is responsible for compliance?
Legally, the duty of care stays with your business. We help keep the paperwork — Duty of Care documentation, transfer notes and records — under control.
How long does it take?
Send the invoice and photos and we'll come back to you with a breakdown. No three-hour consultation, no pressure, no "just sign here."
