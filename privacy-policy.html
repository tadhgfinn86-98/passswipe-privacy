Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

PassSwipe ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how PassSwipe handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

PassSwipe is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

All data generated by the app — including your progress, XP, streaks, session history, and preferences — is stored locally on your device only using your device's standard storage (SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device.

2. In-App Purchases

PassSwipe offers an optional one-time in-app purchase ("PassSwipe Pro") to unlock the full question bank. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not collect, see, or store your payment details.

For information on how Apple or Google handles your payment data, please refer to their respective privacy policies.

3. Local Notifications

With your permission, PassSwipe may send you local push notifications as reminders to practice and to alert you about your league position. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and do not involve any external servers. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

4. No Analytics or Tracking

PassSwipe does not use any analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking technologies. We do not collect crash reports, usage data, or any other telemetry.

5. No Account Required

PassSwipe does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

6. Children's Privacy

PassSwipe does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. As we collect no personal data at all, the app is safe for users of all ages.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
